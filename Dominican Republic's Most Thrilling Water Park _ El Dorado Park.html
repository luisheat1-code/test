Amenities at the park

Additional facilities for your convenience.

Hammocks and Umbrellas

The hammocks and umbrellas are included in the ticket price.

Changing Rooms and Showers

We have changing rooms and showers included in the ticket price.

Lockers

Small lockers $10 and large lockers $15, rented for the day.

Wifi

Free Wi-Fi for customers in some areas of the park.

Towels

Towels are available for rent ($5), or bring your own.

Parking

General parking is included. VIP parking at the gate is $10 per vehicle.
